Type a dish in English or Bahasa. Get the Cantonese name, how to say it, and tap to hear it — so you can tell your employer or an elder exactly what you are cooking.

Audio uses your phone's built-in Cantonese voice. If it sounds like Mandarin, open your device text-to-speech settings and add a Cantonese (香港 / yue) voice. Dictionary is a starter set — more dishes are easy to add.
