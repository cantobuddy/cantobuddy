READ THIS FIRST. Five things decide whether the post lives or dies:
  1. Ask the group admin for permission BEFORE posting. Most HK helper
     groups have an anti-advertising rule and enforce it.
  2. Post as yourself, not as a brand. Don't lead with "CantoBuddy".
  3. Post in Tagalog / Bahasa, not English. English gets scrolled past.
  4. UPLOAD AN IMAGE with the post. Do not rely on the link preview —
     the site's og:image is a small square app icon, so a bare link
     looks like a thumbnail. Put the link in the text or a comment.
  5. Answer every comment. That, not the post, is what grows it.

Image: images/vertical-2-eldercare.png

The four Cantonese words that matter most when you're caring for an
elderly person in Hong Kong 🇭🇰

痛   tung3  —  pain / it hurts
藥   joek6  —  medicine
咳   kat1   —  cough
水   seoi2  —  water

Listen for these four and you'll catch the important half of what she
says. Everything else you can usually work out from context.

I built a free app that teaches exactly this — the Cantonese that
actually gets used at home, with audio and quizzes, in English,
Filipino and Bahasa Indonesia. No cost, no sign-up, works offline.

👉 https://cantobuddy.com/?ref=fb-mix-1

POST D — EMPLOYER / HONG KONG COMMUNITY GROUPS
Image: images/fb-cover.png
=========================================================================

Something for anyone who employs a domestic helper in Hong Kong.

Most helpers arrive speaking English and learn Cantonese on the job —
usually from the person in the household who speaks English least. If
your mother is the one at home all day, she's the one your helper
cannot talk to. That's where misunderstandings start.

I built a free app for this. It teaches the Cantonese that actually
gets used at home — elder care, kitchen, medication, emergencies —
with audio and quizzes, in English, Filipino, Bahasa Indonesia and
Simplified Chinese.

Ten minutes a day is enough to make a real difference.

No cost, no sign-up, works offline:
👉 https://cantobuddy.com/?ref=fb-emp-1

Happy to answer anything about it here.

FIRST WEEK — the order that works
=========================================================================

Day 1    Message the mods of 3-4 groups for permission. Do NOT post yet.
Day 2    Post A in the first Filipino group that said yes. Stay near
         your phone for two hours and answer everything.
Day 3    Post B in an Indonesian group. Same routine.
Day 4-5  Answer the word requests. Add each word to the app, then reply.
Day 6-7  Post C or D in a different group. Ask me for the referral
         numbers — you will already be able to see which group works.
