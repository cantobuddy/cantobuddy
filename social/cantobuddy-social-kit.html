Why social is the SEO plan, not a separate job

Ranking for learn cantonese is not a markup problem — that part is now done. It is an authority problem. Google decides a young domain is worth ranking partly on whether other people link to it and talk about it, and right now almost nobody does.

Every one of these posts is doing two jobs at once:

  • It reaches helpers directly — which is the point of the app.
  • It creates the signals Google reads — links, shares, and people searching "cantobuddy" by name. Branded search is one of the strongest early ranking signals a new site can get, and it only happens if people have heard of you.

The single highest-value thing you can do this month is not another page. It is getting the link in front of 500 real helpers in a Facebook group.

Read this before posting in any group

Helper groups ban promotion, and they are right to. A drive-by "check out my app" link gets you removed, and it burns the group for good. The posts below are written to lead with something genuinely useful and mention the app at the end. Post the helpful content even if nobody clicks. That is what keeps you in the group long enough for it to matter.
  • Read the group rules first. Many allow links only on certain days, or in a specific thread. Follow that.
  • Post the value, not the link. If a post is only a link, it's an ad. If it teaches something, it's a post.
  • Answer replies. A post with 40 comments outranks a post with 40 likes. Ask a question at the end.
  • Don't post the same text in five groups in one hour. Vary it, space it out.
  • Never claim to be a helper if you are not. Post as the person who made it. Honesty survives moderation; a fake testimonial does not.

TikTok scripts

Format: vertical 9:16, 15–35 seconds, hook in the first 2 seconds. Speak slowly and clearly — much of this audience is listening in a second language. Burn the Cantonese into the screen as large text, with Jyutping underneath.

Script 1 — "The one word that does four jobs"

Best performer. One word, four uses, immediately useful.

HOOK (0–2s)  [on screen: 唔該 = m4 goi1]
"If you only learn one Cantonese word in Hong Kong, make it this one."

BODY (2–22s)
唔該 — m4 goi1
[say it twice, slowly]

Watch what it does:
· Someone hands you something → 唔該  (thank you)
· You want to get past → 唔該  (excuse me)
· You need to ask a favour → 唔該  (please)
· You want the bill → 唔該  (could I have...)

One word. Four jobs. English needs four phrases for this.

CTA (22–28s)
[screen: cantobuddy.com — free, in Filipino & Indonesian]
"It's free. Link in bio."

ON-SCREEN TEXT throughout:
唔該 / m4 goi1 / thanks · excuse me · please

CAPTION
The one Cantonese word that does the work of four 🇭🇰 唔該 (m4 goi1). Free app for helpers in the bio — English, Filipino, Bahasa Indonesia.

Script 2 — "What the lola is actually saying"

Relatable-problem hook. This is the one that gets shared.

HOOK (0–2s)  [on screen: "POV: the lola is talking and you have no idea"]
"The hardest part of this job isn't the work. It's this."

BODY (2–25s)
She says something. Fast. You smile and nod.
You have no idea if she asked for water or told you she's in pain.

These four change everything:
[massage?] 揼 — dung2
[pain] 痛 — tung3
[medicine] 藥 — joek6
[water] 水 — seoi2

Listen for these and you'll catch the important half.

"Free app, made for helpers. cantobuddy.com"

ON-SCREEN TEXT

CAPTION

Script 3 — "The six tones in 40 seconds"

Educational. Good for the algorithm — people rewatch it.

HOOK (0–2s)  [on screen: "si1 si2 si3 si4 si5 si6"]
"Six words. Same sound. Completely different meaning."

BODY (2–30s)
[show each one, say it, show the meaning]
si1 詩 — poem
si2 史 — history
si3 試 — to try
si4 時 — time
si5 市 — market
si6 是 — to be

This is why Cantonese sounds impossible.
It isn't. The tone IS part of the word — learn it with the word, not after.

CTA (30–36s)
"Free app. English, Filipino, Bahasa Indonesia. cantobuddy.com"

CAPTION
Why Cantonese sounds impossible (and why it isn't) 🇭🇰 six tones, one syllable. Free app in bio.

Script 4 — "You don't need to be fluent"

Reassurance. Low view ceiling, but the highest conversion to actual installs.

HOOK (0–2s)  [on screen: "You do NOT need to be fluent"]
"Nobody expects you to speak perfect Cantonese. Here's what they do expect."

BODY (2–24s)
You don't need to talk like a local.
You need to understand what's said to you, and say a few things clearly.

That's it. That's the whole job.

Five minutes reading words out loud.
Three minutes on a quiz.
Two minutes picking one word to use today.

Ten minutes. That's the whole method.
