The one Cantonese word that does four jobs 🇭🇰

唔該 (m4 goi1) is the word you'll use most in Hong Kong. It means
"thanks", "excuse me", "please" and "could I have" — all at once.

Why Cantonese sounds impossible (and why it isn't) 🇭🇰

si1 詩 — poem
si2 史 — history
si3 試 — to try
si4 時 — time
si5 市 — market
si6 是 — to be

Same sound. Six meanings. The tone isn't decoration — the tone IS the
word. Learn it with the word, not after.

Free app: cantobuddy.com

THE ONE RULE
=========================================================================

If someone comments asking how to say a specific word — add that word to
the app, then reply to tell them you did. It takes ten minutes, it makes
the app better, and it turns a viewer into someone who tells other
helpers about you.

That loop is the whole growth engine. Everything else is decoration.
