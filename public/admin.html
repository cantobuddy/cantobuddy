Discover feed

Culture posts are written here. News posts are imported from Philippine, Indonesian and Hong Kong public sources (Philippine Daily Inquirer, Antara News and RTHK) as a headline, a short summary and a link — never the full article, which would be a copyright problem.

Each news item is given a topic on import, by keyword — see news-category.js. It is a guess, so it can be corrected on any single post with the Topic field below; Re-classify news only ever fills in topics that are empty and will never overwrite one you have set.

Stories

Stories written by helpers, in their own language. Nothing here is visible to anyone until you approve it. If you add an English gloss, a reader who does not speak her language can follow the story too.

New post

Only used by the News channel. Set it here to correct a wrong guess.

Shown under the headline in the feed and as the page's meta description.

A small HTML subset: <p>, <h2>, <ul>, <ol>, <li>, <strong>, <em>, <a>. Anything else is stripped when it renders. Leave empty for an imported news item — a news post is a headline, a summary and a link, never the article.

Filling these in adds an attribution line and a “Read the full story” link to the article. Required for anything summarising someone else's reporting.
