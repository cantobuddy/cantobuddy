Before you post — the part that decides whether this works

The copy below is the easy part. These five things decide whether the post gets seen, kept, or deleted.

1. Ask permission first, in every group

Most HK helper groups have a rule about advertising, and moderators enforce it. Before you post a link, send the mod a short message. This works:

Hi po! Admin, I made a free Cantonese learning app for helpers — no
ads, no sign-up, no cost. Pwede po bang i-share sa group? If not,
understood po. Thank you! 🙏

Indonesian: Halo Admin, saya membuat aplikasi belajar bahasa Kanton gratis untuk ART — tanpa iklan, tanpa daftar. Boleh saya bagikan di grup? Kalau tidak boleh, tidak masalah. Terima kasih!

A mod who says yes will often pin it. A mod you skipped will ban you and the link gets reported as spam. Same effort, opposite outcome.

2. Post as yourself, not as a brand

Group members can tell the difference instantly, and they respond to the first and ignore the second. You are a person who made something useful — say that. Don't use a logo as your profile picture if you can avoid it, and don't lead with "CantoBuddy".

3. Answer every comment properly

The post is the beginning, not the job. The single highest-value thing you can do is reply to a comment asking "how do you say X?" with the actual answer — and then add that word to the app. See The one rule.

4. Post the image, and don't rely on the link preview

Known limitation — the auto link preview is a small square

The site's og:image is currently the 512×512 app icon, so a bare cantobuddy.com link renders in the feed as a small square thumbnail — much weaker than a photo.

Do this instead: upload one of the prepared images with the post (they are all correctly sized), and put the link in the post text or the first comment. Facebook often shows only the uploaded image and no preview at all, which is exactly what you want.

5. Post in the local language, not English

A Tagalog post in a Filipino group gets read. An English post gets scrolled past, even though everyone can read English. Both versions are below — use the one that matches the group.

Your tagged links — so you can tell which group worked

Every link carries a ?ref= tag. The app records where a learner first arrived from and counts it. So a month from now you can prove which group actually brings people in, instead of guessing.

The comment replies that carry the post

Most of the value is in the comments, not the post. These are the three you will actually get.

"May app na ba nito sa iPhone?" / "Ada versi iOS?"

Yes po! Hindi siya kailangan i-download sa App Store — website siya
na pwede i-save sa home screen:

iPhone: buksan sa Safari → tap ang Share button → "Add to Home
Screen"
Android: buksan sa Chrome → tap ang ⋮ menu → "Add to Home screen"

Tapos parang app na siya — may sariling icon, at gagana offline. 🙏

"Paano sabihin ang [salita]?" / "Bagaimana bilang [kata]?"

Good question! [WORD] po — [JYUTPING]. Ibibigay ko sa [LEVEL] level,
sa [CATEGORY] category. Dinagdag ko na sa app, i-refresh niyo lang
po. Salamat sa suggestion! 🙏
This reply is the whole growth engine. It is the only one that turns a commenter into someone who tells other helpers. Someone who asked for a word, saw it appear, and got a reply is far more likely to share the app than someone who just read a post.

"Scam ba ito? Bakit libre?" / "Ini penipuan?"

Fair question po! Libre siya kasi hindi kailangan ng pera para
patakbuhin — walang ads, walang bayad, at hindi ako humihingi ng
pangalan, email, o numero. Buksan niyo lang at tingnan, walang
kahit anong kailangang i-sign up. Kung may makita kayong
kahit anong hihingi ng bayad, sabihin niyo sa akin agad. 🙏

Indonesian: Pertanyaan bagus! Gratis karena tidak butuh biaya untuk menjalankannya — tanpa iklan, tanpa bayar, dan saya tidak meminta nama, email, atau nomor telepon. Silakan dibuka dan dilihat, tidak perlu daftar apa pun.

The one rule

If someone comments asking how to say a specific word — add that word to the app, then reply to tell them you did.

It takes ten minutes, it makes the app better, and it turns a viewer into someone who tells other helpers about you.

This is also why the app is a good fit for group marketing at all: helpers ask for words constantly and for free. Every one of those questions is a piece of content you did not have to think of, and an excuse to comment again on your own post — which pushes it back up the group feed.

Suggested order for the first week

DayDo
1Message the mods of 3–4 groups for permission. Do not post yet.
2Post A in the first Filipino group that said yes. Stay near your phone for two hours and answer everything.
3Post B in an Indonesian group. Same routine.
4–5Answer the word requests. Add each word to the app, then reply.
6–7Post C or D in a different group. Ask me for the referral numbers — you will already be able to see which group is working.

Two things to know before you start.

1. The vocabulary in the app is machine-drafted and has not yet been checked by a native Cantonese speaker. If someone in a comment corrects a word, that is genuinely useful — take it, and fix it in the app.

2. Nothing here costs anything and nothing collects personal data. If anyone in a group claims otherwise, that is worth knowing about — tell me.
