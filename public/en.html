CantoBuddy 學英文 — Learn Everyday English

CantoBuddy 學英文 helps elderly people in Hong Kong learn simple, everyday English for home life: household items room by room, housework instructions for the helper, and shopping for daily goods. Each word is shown with its Cantonese meaning and spoken aloud in both English and Cantonese.

Browse the English words by topic:

  • English for Daily Life — all topics
  • Greetings
  • Numbers & Money
  • Kitchen
  • Shopping Goods
  • Emergency

The free English track also has a short daily read: original jokes, everyday life tips (生活小常識), classic Chinese wisdom stories (中國智慧故事) and short notes to help a family understand their domestic helper. Meanings can be read in Traditional or Simplified Chinese. There is also a help directory of verified Hong Kong support numbers and large emoji cards that can be shared to WhatsApp or Facebook with one tap.

Four rotating home banners link directly to games (遊戲), everyday life tips (生活小常識), helper perspectives (姐姐心聲) and finding help (搵幫手).

Also learn Cantonese for free at CantoBuddy, including Cantonese for domestic helpers and Cantonese food phrases.
